About DSCET

Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering & Technology (DSCET), located on the scenic East Coast Road in Mamallapuram, is a hub of innovation, learning, and growth. Since its inception in 2001 with just 150 students, DSCET has grown remarkably, now offering 16 undergraduate and 7 postgraduate programs with an annual intake of 1,032 students.

Approved by AICTE, affiliated to Anna University, and accredited with NAAC ‘A+’ and NBA, the institution stands for excellence in education. With permanent affiliation for CSE, ECE, EEE, MECH, IT, and MBA, DSCET empowers students to become leaders of tomorrow.

Vision & Mission

Our Mission

To provide quality education that transforms students into skilled professionals and responsible citizens through innovative teaching, research, and industry collaboration.

Our Vision

To become a world-class hub of innovation, learning, and research, shaping students into global achievers who meet and exceed national and international standards. .
