About the Department

Information Technology is the backbone of the modern information age, seamlessly connecting businesses and communities worldwide. Our department blends computing expertise with managerial insights and social understanding to develop innovative IT solutions that address today's challenges and anticipate future needs.

Located in Chennai—the intellectual capital of IT—our program prepares students with a robust curriculum focused on practical applications in business problem-solving, cutting-edge research, and industry-relevant skills. Graduates from our program enjoy excellent career prospects globally in all sectors where information and communication technologies are pivotal.

Program Outcomes (POs)

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply knowledge of mathematics, science, and IT engineering fundamentals to solve complex problems.
  • Analyze and research complex engineering problems using scientific principles.
  • Design effective solutions considering safety, societal, and environmental impacts.
  • Conduct research and experiments, analyze, and synthesize data for valid conclusions.
  • Use modern engineering and IT tools with an understanding of their limitations.
  • Assess societal, legal, health, and ethical implications in engineering practice.
  • Promote sustainable development through responsible engineering solutions.
  • Commit to professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Collaborate effectively as individuals and team members across disciplines.
  • Communicate technical ideas clearly through reports and presentations.
  • Apply project management and financial principles in complex environments.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to adapt to technological evolution.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Problem Solving

Identify, formulate, and solve complex problems by applying knowledge in mathematics, science, and IT.

Technical Excellence

Analyze, design, implement, and manage software projects using modern tools and continuous learning.

Professionalism

Demonstrate professionalism, ethical integrity, strong communication, teamwork and contribute meaningfully to society.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

Software Development

Design and develop software solutions using logical problem-solving, user interfaces, and backend database connectivity with modern object-oriented programming.

Application Development

Create, test, and deploy mobile and web applications, gaining skills that enhance employability and entrepreneurship.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To produce highly skilled technologists and researchers who drive the nation to new heights in Information Technology research and industry, while providing leadership in technical education and innovation.

Mission

To evolve as a Centre of Excellence in Information Technology, delivering quality engineering education at the undergraduate level, nurturing the overall personality of students, instilling ethical values, and preparing them to meet the dynamic challenges of the IT industry, research organizations, and the diverse needs of society.
