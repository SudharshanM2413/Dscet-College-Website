About the Course

The MBA program is a four-semester, two-year full-time program that follows the Anna University curriculum, regularly updated to meet dynamic industry requirements. The faculty of our institution actively contribute to curriculum development, ensuring that academic content remains relevant and impactful.

The teaching methodology blends modern pedagogy with practical exposure through:

  • Case studies
  • Group discussions
  • Role plays
  • Business simulations & games
  • Workshops and industry interactions

Technology plays a vital role in the learning process, supported by the Graduate Academic Resource Center (GARC), a centralized academic repository.

Student evaluation includes continuous internal assessments and university examinations. Regular internal tests ensure consistent learning and performance tracking.

Students also undertake real-time industry projects and internships in their chosen specialization, making them "industry-ready" graduates. Additionally, elective-based projects further enhance applied knowledge, problem-solving skills, and entrepreneurial thinking.

Program Outcomes

On successful completion of the MBA program, graduates will be able to:

  • PO1: Apply management theories and practices to effectively solve business problems.
  • PO2: Demonstrate analytical and critical thinking for data-driven decision making.
  • PO3: Exhibit leadership qualities grounded in values and ethics.
  • PO4: Understand, analyze, and communicate global, economic, legal, and ethical aspects of business.
  • PO5: Lead themselves and others in achieving organizational goals while contributing effectively in team environments.
  • PO6: Continuously upgrade professional and managerial skills in the workplace.
  • PO7: Take up challenging assignments with confidence.
  • PO8: Pursue lifelong learning for personal and professional growth.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be recognized as a Centre of Excellence in Management Studies, developing socially responsible leaders, aspiring managers, and entrepreneurs through holistic education, research, and industry collaboration.

Mission

M1: Develop knowledge-driven, inspiring professionals, competent managers, and efficient leaders dedicated to the growth of business and society.

M2: Foster strong collaboration with industry to provide students with opportunities aligned with real-world requirements and professional expertise.

M3: Instill moral, ethical, and social values in students, enabling them to uphold human commitments and contribute meaningfully to society.
