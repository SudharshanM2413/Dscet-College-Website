About the Department

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at DSCET is equipped with excellent infrastructure and a dedicated faculty team that inspires innovation and creativity. Our research spans diverse technical areas including:

  • Acoustics and noise control
  • Air conditioning and refrigeration
  • Automatic controls and robotics
  • Computer-aided design (CAD)
  • Energy management and sustainability
  • Fluid dynamics and turbo-machinery
  • Biomechanics and medical devices

We blend classroom theory with hands-on projects to prepare future-ready engineers. Through memberships with professional bodies like SAE and ISHREA, we conduct technical symposiums, national workshops, and conferences to enhance both technical and interpersonal skills.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex mechanical problems.
  • Analyze, research, and formulate solutions using first principles and scientific methods.
  • Design systems and processes mindful of safety, societal, and environmental impacts.
  • Conduct research-based investigations, experiments, and data analysis to yield valid conclusions.
  • Utilize modern engineering tools and IT techniques with an understanding of their limitations.
  • Evaluate societal, legal, health, and ethical considerations in engineering practice.
  • Promote sustainable and environmentally responsible engineering solutions.
  • Uphold professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Collaborate effectively individually and as team leaders in multidisciplinary projects.
  • Communicate complex engineering concepts clearly and professionally.
  • Manage projects efficiently using engineering and management principles.
  • Embrace lifelong learning to adapt to technological advancements.

Program Educational Objectives

Strong Foundation

Build a strong foundation in mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve engineering challenges and pursue advanced studies.

Design & Improvement

Develop skills in designing, improving, and installing integrated mechanical systems involving people and materials.

Modern Practices

Gain knowledge of modern mechanical system design and industrial practices for better employability and adaptability.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To nurture technically competent Mechanical Engineering professionals capable of delivering multifaceted services, driving innovation and advancement in engineering and technology, while upholding professional ethics and addressing societal needs.

Mission

M1: Provide world-class education that equips students for successful careers in mechanical engineering and inspires aspirations for entrepreneurship.

M2: Achieve excellence in research and innovation by advancing mechanical engineering through emerging and cutting-edge technologies.

M3: Strengthen collaboration with industries and establish strategic partnerships for mutual growth and knowledge exchange.

M4: Instill strong ethical values and social responsibility in students, empowering them to contribute meaningfully to society.
