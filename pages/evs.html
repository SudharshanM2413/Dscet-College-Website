About the Department

Established in 2010, the Department of Environmental Science & Technology is dedicated to creating environmental professionals who can tackle pressing global challenges through innovative solutions.

Our program offers:

  • B.Tech. (4 years) — A comprehensive curriculum integrating environmental science with technological applications
  • Interdisciplinary approach combining chemistry, biology, engineering, and policy

Our faculty members bring diverse expertise from industry and research, providing students with hands-on training in cutting-edge environmental technologies and methodologies.

Students gain proficiency in industry-standard software and tools: GIS, Environmental Modeling Software, Water Quality Analysis Tools, and Remote Sensing Applications.

Program Outcomes

Our graduates gain the ability to:

  • Apply scientific principles to environmental problem-solving
  • Design and implement sustainable environmental technologies
  • Conduct environmental impact assessments and monitoring
  • Develop waste management and pollution control strategies
  • Communicate environmental information effectively to diverse audiences
  • Understand environmental policy, regulations, and ethical considerations

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a center of excellence in environmental education and research, producing competent professionals committed to developing sustainable solutions for environmental challenges and contributing to the well-being of society and the planet.

Mission

M1: To provide comprehensive education that integrates environmental science with technology, preparing students for successful careers.

M2: To foster research and innovation in environmental protection, conservation, and sustainable development.

M3: To promote environmental awareness, ethical practices, and community engagement for a sustainable future.
