About the Department

The Department of Food Technology aims to develop skilled professionals ready to meet the demands of the rapidly growing food processing industry. Alongside nurturing technical expertise, the department supports farmers and entrepreneurs in establishing small-scale food businesses.

The curriculum covers vital areas including food processing, preservation, chemistry, quality control, safety, and marketing. Designed to prepare industry-ready graduates, the program also fosters entrepreneurial skills, enabling students to excel in research, teaching, and the food sector.

With expanding opportunities in India's booming food processing industry, graduates find careers in diverse roles—handling purchase, storage, transportation, distribution, quality management, and innovation in food products and technologies.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering principles to solve complex food technology problems.
  • Analyze and investigate engineering challenges using research methodologies and data interpretation.
  • Design innovative, safe, and sustainable food processing systems and products.
  • Utilize modern engineering and IT tools with an understanding of their scope and limitations.
  • Consider societal, environmental, and ethical responsibilities in professional practice.
  • Work effectively individually and in multidisciplinary teams with strong communication skills.
  • Manage projects successfully using engineering and management principles.
  • Commit to lifelong learning adapting to continual technological change.

Program Specific Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply process engineering fundamentals to develop innovative food formulations and commercial products.
  • Develop and optimize food processing techniques and tools for both laboratory and mass production.
  • Design hygienic, time-saving, and labor-efficient food processing equipment to ensure quality and safety.

Vision & Mission

Mission
