PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

Welcome to Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering and Technology (DSCET)—an institution dedicated to shaping future-ready professionals and leaders in Engineering and Management.

Our vision is to be a world-class center of advanced learning, research, and innovation, preparing students to meet national and global standards. At DSCET, education is not just about knowledge transfer—it is about nurturing talent, fostering creativity, and building confidence to face the challenges of tomorrow.

We take pride in our modern infrastructure, state-of-the-art facilities, committed faculty, and outcome-based teaching methods. Our academic ecosystem is designed to provide industry-relevant knowledge, hands-on training, and real-world exposure, ensuring that our graduates are equipped with both competence and character.

Innovative

Progress comes from fresh ideas and bold thinking

Relevant

Preparing students to address pressing societal needs

Sustainable

Endurance and adaptability ensure growth in a changing world

Beyond academics, our vibrant campus life fosters cultural harmony, creativity, and holistic growth. Located in Mamallapuram—a historic center of art and culture—our students find themselves in an environment that inspires learning, exploration, and excellence.

Dear students, I encourage you to dream big, think differently, and work with determination. At DSCET, you will not only build your career but also shape yourself into a responsible individual ready to contribute meaningfully to society and the world.

– Dr. SURESH G.R., M.E., Ph.D.
