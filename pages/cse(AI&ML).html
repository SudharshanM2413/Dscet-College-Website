About the Department

Started in 2023, our department offers a cutting-edge 4-year B.E. program in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML). Our experienced faculty bring rich industrial and academic expertise, driving strong research and innovation. We blend traditional teaching with ICT-enhanced techniques to equip students for the future.

The program trains students to harness AI and ML technologies that are revolutionizing industries—from healthcare and manufacturing to finance and retail—improving efficiencies and augmenting human capabilities. Students explore a broad spectrum of AI/ML concepts, tools, and applications, gaining hands-on experience with advanced AI tools widely used in the industry.

AI & ML focus on building intelligent systems to perform tasks that once required human intellect. These technologies transform banking, manufacturing, automotive, cybersecurity, healthcare, and more, shaping the future of technology and daily life.

Program Outcomes (POs)

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering knowledge to solve complex AI & ML problems.
  • Identify and analyze complex engineering problems using foundational principles and research.
  • Design AI & ML solutions with attention to public health, safety, societal, and environmental impacts.
  • Use research methods and experiment design to analyze and interpret data effectively.
  • Utilize modern AI tools and IT techniques for modeling and problem-solving within limitations.
  • Consider societal, legal, cultural, and ethical aspects in professional AI engineering.
  • Promote sustainable development with environmentally conscious solutions.
  • Commit to professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Work effectively individually and in diverse, multidisciplinary teams.
  • Communicate complex ideas clearly across technical and societal domains.
  • Manage projects and collaborate within teams using engineering and management principles.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to adapt to evolving AI technologies.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To emerge as a Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, fostering a cognitive learning environment through collaboration with academia, research, and industry, while addressing global challenges with innovation and sustainability.

Mission

M1: Prepare next-generation professionals skilled in intelligent data analysis and capable of designing systems that emulate human intelligence.

M2: Develop practically trained experts through effective, innovative, and industry-aligned teaching-learning methodologies.

M3: Nurture professionals with advanced problem-solving abilities, enabling them to design intelligent solutions for real-world challenges.
