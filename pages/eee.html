About the Department

From Edison's invention of the light bulb to today's smart systems, Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) is at the heart of modern technology. This discipline explores the fundamentals of electrons and develops solutions for a vast array of applications—from electrical equipment and radar to medical devices, navigation systems, and computers.

Our program equips students to design, develop, test, and manufacture cutting-edge electrical and electronic systems. Graduates are prepared for diverse roles involving specification, development, implementation, and research to innovate and improve technology.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex problems.
  • Identify, analyze, and research complex engineering challenges and develop substantiated solutions.
  • Design and develop solutions considering public health, safety, societal, and environmental impacts.
  • Conduct investigations using research-based methods for valid conclusions.
  • Utilize modern tools and IT techniques effectively with an understanding of their limitations.
  • Assess the societal, legal, and ethical implications of engineering solutions responsibly.
  • Promote sustainability and environmentally conscious engineering.
  • Uphold professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Work effectively as individuals and team leaders in multidisciplinary projects.
  • Communicate technical information clearly to diverse audiences.
  • Apply project management and financial principles in complex engineering tasks.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to keep pace with technological advancements.

Career Opportunities

Graduates of our programs are ready to contribute in various sectors including:

  • Power Generation and Distribution
  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • Industrial Automation and Control
  • Electrical Equipment Manufacturing
  • Electronics Design and Development
  • Research and Development Organizations
  • Consulting and Engineering Services
  • Defense and Aerospace Industries

From power systems to electronic devices, from conventional energy to renewable solutions, our students step out as innovation-driven professionals ready to take on the challenges of the electrical and electronics world.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To impart knowledge in Electrical Science and Technology with advanced learning that meets international standards, while nurturing ethically and emotionally strong professionals capable of addressing technological challenges for the welfare of the nation in the years to come.

Mission

M1: Provide a conducive learning environment that enhances student knowledge through diverse and effective learning methodologies.

M2: Promote social awareness and equip students with cutting-edge technologies to meet industrial demands and contribute to societal needs.
