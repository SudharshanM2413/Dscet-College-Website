About the Department

Launched in 2007, the Department of Aeronautical Engineering has been shaping next-generation aerospace professionals with a perfect blend of theory, practice, and innovation.

We offer:

  • B.E. (4 years) — a comprehensive program covering all core aspects of aeronautical engineering.
  • M.E. (2 years) — advanced specialization for future innovators in the aerospace field.

With a team of highly qualified faculty bringing strong industry and research exposure, students benefit from modern teaching methods, ICT tools, and continuous hands-on training.

Students also master industry-leading software: CATIA, ANSYS, HYPERMESH, FLUENT.

Program Outcomes

Our graduates gain the ability to:

  • Solve complex engineering problems with strong fundamentals.
  • Model, design, and simulate aircraft components.
  • Apply aerodynamics & propulsion knowledge to real-world challenges.
  • Work effectively in teams and manage projects in aerospace.
  • Communicate professionally with the global aviation community.
  • Embrace ethics, sustainability, and lifelong learning in aviation.

Career Scope

Graduates of our programs are ready to contribute in:

  • Aircraft Design & Manufacturing
  • Aircraft Maintenance & Systems
  • Defense & Aerospace R&D
  • Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, Drones, Helicopters, Gliders
  • Avionics & Flight Simulation

From airplanes to UAVs, from design to maintenance, our students step out as innovation-driven professionals ready to take on the challenges of the aerospace world.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To elevate technically competent aeronautical engineering professionals to Perpetrated multifaceted service for achieving improvement and innovation with sustainable engineering education, social responsibility and research skills to contribute for the national and global development.

Mission

M1: To furnish world class education that fitting students for a better carrier in aeronautical engineering and aspiration for entrepreneurship.

M2: To obtain excellence in research towards Advancement in the field of aeronautical engineering with cutting edge technologies.

M3: To cultivate the students with ethical and social values for contributing towards the upliftment of the society making this world a better place for living.
