About the Department

Established in 2012 and approved by AICTE, the Department of Civil Engineering is committed to providing students with deep exposure to diverse fields within civil engineering. Our well-equipped labs and modern facilities create an ideal learning environment to nurture technical expertise.

We have a team of qualified and experienced faculty, complemented by frequent interactions with eminent academicians and industry experts, ensuring students stay updated on the latest global advancements. The department actively organizes seminars, expert talks, aptitude tests, quizzes, field visits, and career counseling to develop students' technical knowledge alongside interpersonal skills.

Program Outcomes

Graduates of the program will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex civil engineering problems.
  • Analyze, review, and synthesize research literature to resolve engineering challenges using fundamental principles.
  • Design solutions and systems considering public health, safety, societal, cultural, and environmental factors.
  • Conduct investigations using research-based methods, experiments, and data analysis to reach valid conclusions.
  • Employ modern engineering tools and IT techniques for prediction, modeling, and analysis with awareness of limitations.
  • Evaluate societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural contexts in professional engineering practice.
  • Champion sustainable development by considering environmental impacts.
  • Adhere to professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Work effectively as individuals and team members in interdisciplinary settings.
  • Communicate clearly through reports, documentation, and presentations with professional audiences and society.
  • Understand and apply project management and finance principles in multidisciplinary projects.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to keep pace with technological and industrial changes.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To evolve as a Centre of Excellence in Civil Engineering education, shaping competent professionals who contribute to sustainable development and the betterment of humanity.

Mission

M1: Provide high-quality education and hands-on training to nurture skilled and innovative civil engineers.

M2: Strengthen collaboration with industries and research organizations to build a strong knowledge base in current and emerging civil engineering technologies.

M3: Instill ethical values, professional responsibility, and social commitment in students to prepare them as civil engineers dedicated to societal progress.
