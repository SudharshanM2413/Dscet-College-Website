About the Department

Established in 2019, the Department of Biomedical Engineering offers a comprehensive undergraduate program focused on integrating engineering principles with biological sciences. Our dedicated faculty bring expertise across interdisciplinary research areas, fostering innovation and practical learning.

Well-equipped labs provide students with ample opportunities to deepen their scientific knowledge and hands-on skills. The department's active R&D teams contribute to government and industry-funded projects, giving students valuable exposure to cutting-edge research and real-world applications.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply advanced knowledge in mathematics, science, and engineering to solve complex biomedical problems.
  • Analyze and interpret complex engineering challenges using first principles and research methods.
  • Design innovative solutions while considering safety, society, and environmental impact.
  • Conduct investigations, experiments, and analyze data to draw valid conclusions.
  • Use modern tools and IT resources to model and predict biomedical systems, understanding their limitations.
  • Assess societal, ethical, legal, and health implications of engineering practices.
  • Promote sustainable and environmentally responsible engineering solutions.
  • Uphold professional ethics and responsibilities.
  • Collaborate effectively as individuals and team members in multidisciplinary settings.
  • Communicate complex ideas clearly through reports, presentations, and documentation.
  • Manage engineering projects efficiently within diverse teams.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to stay current with technological advancements.

Program Educational Objectives

Apply Core Knowledge

Apply core engineering and biological knowledge to solve real-world biomedical challenges.

Demonstrate Leadership

Demonstrate leadership, ethical decision-making, and effective communication in multidisciplinary environments.

Pursue Lifelong Learning

Pursue lifelong learning to continuously expand their technical expertise and adapt to evolving technologies.

Program Specific Outcomes

Graduates will be equipped to:

  • Design and develop diagnostic and therapeutic devices that reduce physician workload and improve patient care.
  • Develop software algorithms targeting healthcare challenges across medical sectors.
  • Innovate with emerging ICT technologies to create indigenous medical instruments that meet global standards.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be recognized as a world-leading platform in Biomedical Engineering, distinguished by the excellence of our people, innovative multidisciplinary research, and discovery-driven educational programs. We aspire to pioneer the translation of biomedical engineering research into transformative applications that advance healthcare and improve the quality of life worldwide.

Mission

Our mission is to build and sustain a vibrant biomedical engineering community at local, national, and global levels by:

  • Communicating recent advances, discoveries, and innovations in the field.
  • Promoting education, research, and professional development.
  • Integrating perspectives from academia, medicine, government, and industry.
  • Driving collaborations that bridge science, technology, and healthcare for societal benefit.
