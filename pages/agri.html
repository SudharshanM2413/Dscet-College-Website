About the Department

Established in 2019, the Department of Agricultural Engineering is dedicated to shaping skilled professionals for the fast-evolving world of agriculture and farming technology. From the start, the department has delivered strong infrastructure, modern computing facilities, and an inspiring academic environment.

Students get world-class training, hands-on research experiences, and strong mentorship from highly qualified faculty. Our focus on teamwork, technical skills, and real industry insight empowers students to thrive and adapt in the ever-changing agri-sector.

Program Outcomes

Graduates of the program will be able to:

  • Apply advanced knowledge in math, science, and engineering to tackle complex agricultural challenges.
  • Identify and analyze real-life agricultural problems using cutting-edge research and technology.
  • Design effective, safe, and sustainable solutions keeping public health, society, and environment in mind.
  • Conduct investigations and interpret research data to make well-founded decisions.
  • Master modern tools, modeling, and IT applications relevant to agricultural engineering.
  • Assess technological solutions in terms of social, cultural, legal, and environmental impacts.
  • Champion sustainable development through responsible engineering practices.
  • Uphold professional ethics and industry standards.
  • Work efficiently both as individuals and as team members in multidisciplinary settings.
  • Communicate ideas clearly through reports, presentations, and effective documentation.
  • Apply project management and financial knowledge to lead and support various projects.
  • Embrace a habit of life-long learning for ongoing success in fast-changing technology.

Program Educational Objectives

Industry Careers

Build careers in tractor & implement manufacturing, seed processing, irrigation & drainage companies, or launch their own ventures such as dairy farms and custom hiring centers.

Higher Studies

Pursue higher studies and drive innovation in agricultural water management, farm machinery, and energy systems.

Ethical Professionals

Understand and uphold ethics, safety, professionalism, diversity, globalization, and environmental responsibility as proactive contributors to society.

Program Specific Outcomes

Diverse Career Opportunities

Secure jobs across diverse domains of agricultural engineering, leveraging broad technical expertise.

Resource Management

Optimize natural resource management using modern soil and water conservation and irrigation techniques.

Farm Machinery Expertise

Design, assess, and solve problems involving farm machinery and power for sustainable agriculture.

Food Technology

Apply deep technical knowledge to boost food quality, develop value-added agri-technologies, and excel in quality control.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To develop skilled and innovative Agricultural Engineers equipped with comprehensive knowledge, technical expertise, and a spirit of service, contributing effectively to agricultural research, sustainable practices, and the nation's growth.

Mission

M1: Deliver excellence in teaching by providing quality professional education enriched with ethical and moral values.

M2: Produce technically competent engineers who can excel in research, innovation, and agricultural advancements, addressing present and future global challenges.
