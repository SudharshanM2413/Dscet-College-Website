About the Course

The Department of MCA was established in 2007 with a team of highly experienced faculty members. The department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and a well-stocked library with the latest journals and resources.

To prepare students for the industry, placement-oriented training is provided, along with seminars conducted by senior executives from reputed organizations and academic institutions. Industrial visits to multinational companies are regularly organized to provide exposure to corporate environments and industry practices.

Program Objective

The primary objective of the MCA program is to provide a quality learning environment that inspires students through:

Experienced Faculty

Experienced and motivating faculty

Industry Experts

Knowledge sharing from industry and academic experts

Growth Opportunities

Opportunities to grow in the field of Computer Applications

The program aims to develop professionals with a blend of technical skills, creativity, and research orientation.

Program Outcomes

On successful completion of the MCA program, graduates will be able to:

  • PO1: Apply knowledge of mathematics, science, and computer fundamentals to solve complex computing problems.
  • PO2: Identify, analyze, and review research literature to develop innovative solutions for real-world challenges.
  • PO3: Design and develop software systems, applications, and processes that meet societal, environmental, and industrial needs.
  • PO4: Conduct investigations and research using modern methods, data analysis, and experimental design.
  • PO5: Select and apply appropriate tools, techniques, and modern IT resources with an awareness of their limitations.
  • PO6: Understand professional and societal responsibilities, applying contextual knowledge in practice.
  • PO7: Demonstrate awareness of environmental and sustainability considerations in computing solutions.
  • PO8: Uphold ethical standards and professional responsibilities.
  • PO9: Work effectively as individuals, team members, and leaders in multidisciplinary environments.
  • PO10: Communicate effectively in technical and professional contexts through documentation, presentations, and collaboration.
  • PO11: Apply principles of project management and finance to lead teams and manage projects efficiently.
  • PO12: Recognize the need for lifelong learning to adapt to technological advancements.
