About the Department

Since its establishment in 2001, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) has been dedicated to educating students from across India and abroad, including those from rural backgrounds, empowering them to uplift their families, industries, and society.

Our program emphasizes a thorough understanding of computer fundamentals, complemented by hands-on experience on diverse hardware and software platforms. Beyond the university syllabus, students explore cutting-edge technologies and real-world applications, preparing them for dynamic tech careers.

Program Outcomes

Graduates of the program will be able to:

  • Apply core knowledge in mathematics, science, and engineering to solve complex computing problems.
  • Analyze and research intricate engineering challenges using fundamental principles.
  • Design and develop solutions considering public health, safety, and societal impacts.
  • Conduct experiments, analyze data, and synthesize findings for valid conclusions.
  • Leverage modern tools and IT resources for prediction, modeling, and problem-solving.
  • Evaluate social, legal, ethical, and cultural aspects within engineering practices.
  • Promote sustainable and environmentally responsible technical solutions.
  • Uphold professional ethics and commitments.
  • Collaborate and lead effectively in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Communicate complex concepts clearly and professionally.
  • Apply project management and financial knowledge in varied technical projects.
  • Pursue continuous learning to stay current in rapidly evolving technology fields.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To impart cutting-edge knowledge and skills in Computer Science and Engineering, fostering advanced learning that aligns with global standards. We aim to nurture ethically grounded, emotionally resilient, and innovative professionals capable of addressing evolving technological challenges and contributing meaningfully to the nation's progress and welfare in the years ahead.

Mission

M1: To provide exceptional teaching through a comprehensive curriculum that integrates professional excellence with strong ethical and moral values.

M2: To develop technically proficient computer engineers equipped to excel in research, innovation, software development, and other emerging fields, thereby driving technological advancement and societal benefit.
