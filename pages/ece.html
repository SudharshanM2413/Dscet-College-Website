About the Department

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is a dynamic field focused on the analysis, design, and application of systems that capture, process, store, display, and transmit information. With rapid advancements in the communication industry, our program prepares students for diverse and exciting careers in design, development, and production support.

We offer:

  • B.E. (4 years) — a comprehensive program covering all core aspects of electronics and communication engineering
  • M.E. Communication Systems (2 years) — advanced specialization for future innovators in communication technologies

With a team of highly qualified faculty bringing strong industry and research exposure, students benefit from modern teaching methods, ICT tools, and continuous hands-on training.

Program Outcomes (POs)

Graduates will be able to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex engineering problems.
  • Analyze and formulate software and hardware problems for effective solutions.
  • Design systems, components, or processes promoting economic growth, environmental safety, and societal welfare.
  • Conduct research-based investigations and draw valid conclusions.
  • Use modern IT tools and techniques effectively while understanding their limits.
  • Assess societal and ethical impacts of engineering solutions responsibly.
  • Champion sustainable and environmentally conscious engineering practices.
  • Uphold professional and ethical responsibilities.
  • Collaborate effectively as individuals, leaders, and team members in multidisciplinary settings.
  • Communicate complex concepts clearly and professionally.
  • Apply engineering and management principles to projects in various environments.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to keep skills aligned with technological progress.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To produce disciplined, knowledgeable, and industry-ready engineers in Electronics and Communication, capable of shaping the future world with innovation, integrity, and excellence.

Mission

M1: Attain global recognition and excellence in the field of Electronics and Communication Engineering through quality education and research.

M2: Foster a multidisciplinary learning approach that connects engineering concepts with broader social, ethical, and environmental contexts.

M3: Equip students with essential knowledge, technical skills, and innovative thinking to enhance their employability and entrepreneurial potential.
