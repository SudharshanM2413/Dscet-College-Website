Governing Council - Connecting Management, Staff, Students and Community

Governance is the key activity that connects between the management, staff, students and the community. We believe it should be effective, efficient and economical in execution of its duties.

We support modern governance and proper administration and believe these should be carried out in a way that actively acknowledges diversity, that is respectful of identity and serious belief and that reflects balance.

Academic Council - Shaping Academic Excellence

Academic Council

The Academic Council is responsible for maintaining the standards of teaching, research, and academic excellence in the institution. It plays a pivotal role in shaping the academic direction of the college and ensuring quality education.

Members of the Committee

# Member Name Member Position Designation
1 Dr.SURESH.G.R Principal Coordinator
2 Dr.E.Baraneetharan / Dr.K.Radhika Associate Professor,EEE / Associate Professor,MECH Member
3 Dr.Gayathri.R Professor, Dept of PHYSICS Member
4 HODs of all departments Heads of Department Member

Functions of the Committee

  • To frame the vision and mission of the college along with the POs and PEOs
  • Identification of curricular gaps using input from the stakeholders and suggest necessary actions.
  • Validate the department vision, mission and PEO.
  • To monitor the academic performances of the staff members by going through their lesson plans and log books.
  • To organize periodical meetings to assess the effectiveness of teaching and discuss about the improvements to be made in the curricular and co-curricular aspects.
