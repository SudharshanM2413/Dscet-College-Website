About the Department

Established in 2022, the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Engineering offers a comprehensive 4-year B.Tech program designed to equip students with the tools and knowledge driving the future of technology.

Our experienced faculty, with rich industrial and academic backgrounds, bring a strong research and development culture to the department, enhanced by ICT-based teaching methods for a modern learning experience.

This program prepares students to harness the power of intelligent machines and data analytics, shaping breakthroughs across healthcare, finance, manufacturing, retail, and more. Students embark on a journey through cutting-edge AI technologies transforming industries, improving efficiencies, and augmenting human capabilities.

The curriculum builds strong foundations in basic sciences, humanities, and engineering, alongside core AI and Data Science subjects. Students gain hands-on training in advanced AI and data science tools widely adopted across companies.

AI & DS is an interdisciplinary field spanning science, engineering, and technology, impacting all sectors of industry, academia, and research. Key topics include:

  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Fundamentals of AI
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • Optimization
  • Big Data Engineering
  • Data Visualization
  • Responsible AI
  • Reforcement Learning
  • Time Series Analysis

Program Outcomes

Graduates of the program will be able to:

  • Apply foundational knowledge in mathematics, science, and engineering to solve complex problems.
  • Analyze and evaluate complex AI and data challenges using research-based methods.
  • Design safe, efficient, and socially responsible AI and data-driven solutions.
  • Conduct investigations and interpret data to provide valid conclusions.
  • Use modern AI tools and IT techniques effectively with an understanding of their limits.
  • Address societal, ethical, legal, and environmental impacts of engineering solutions.
  • Commit to sustainable development and professional ethics.
  • Collaborate in diverse, multidisciplinary teams.
  • Communicate complex technical concepts clearly through reports and presentations.
  • Manage projects involving AI and data science in team settings.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to keep up with evolving technologies.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To emerge as a Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, fostering a cognitive learning environment through strong collaboration with academia, research, and industry, while addressing global challenges with innovation and sustainability.

Mission

M1: Nurture the next generation of professionals equipped with expertise in intelligent data analysis and the ability to design systems that emulate human intelligence.

M2: Deliver practically trained professionals through innovative and effective teaching-learning methodologies, bridging theory with real-world applications.

M3: Cultivate highly skilled professionals with problem-solving abilities, capable of developing intelligent solutions to address real-time societal and industrial challenges.
