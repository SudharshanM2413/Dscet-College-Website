About the Department

Our B.E. Cyber Security program is designed to meet the growing industry demand for skilled security professionals across both data security and network/cloud security domains. The curriculum combines core computer science fundamentals with specialized courses in cyber security.

Students gain comprehensive knowledge and hands-on skills to:

  • Secure computer systems and networks
  • Detect and analyze cyber threats
  • Respond effectively to attacks
  • Develop robust security policies and standards

The program prepares graduates to safeguard digital assets in a rapidly evolving cyber landscape with a team of highly qualified faculty bringing strong industry and research exposure.

Program Outcomes

Our graduates gain the ability to:

  • Apply mathematics, science, and engineering knowledge to solve complex cyber security problems.
  • Analyze, research, and formulate solutions for intricate security challenges.
  • Design secure systems and processes addressing public safety, legal, cultural, and environmental factors.
  • Conduct rigorous investigations and data analysis to draw valid conclusions.
  • Use modern engineering and IT tools for prediction, modeling, and problem-solving.
  • Assess societal and ethical implications related to cyber security engineering.
  • Promote sustainable and responsible security solutions.
  • Commit to professional ethics and standards.
  • Collaborate effectively in individual and team-based, multidisciplinary settings.
  • Communicate technical information clearly and professionally.
  • Apply project management and leadership skills in diverse environments.
  • Engage in lifelong learning to keep pace with evolving cyber threats and technologies.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To ignite and nurture young learners with a sustainable, humane, and research-driven educational platform in the domain of Cybersecurity. The department aspires to evolve as a Centre of Excellence in academics and human resource development, leveraging advanced security tools to foster cyber awareness and promote a safer digital world.

Mission

M1: Provide dedicated faculty, robust infrastructure, and a strong theoretical and practical foundation in cybersecurity to empower the next generation of professionals.

M2: Establish collaborations through MoUs and Centres of Excellence (CoEs) with the IT industry, producing industry-ready cybersecurity graduates with research aptitude and a vision for sustainable development.
